About me

I'm an electrical engineering undergrad at Georgia Tech, focused on analog mixed-signal design. I'm especially into analog IC design and where it meets audio, music, and machine learning — including neuromorphic analog circuits.

Outside the lab I play guitar, produce music, play basketball, and speedsolve Rubik's cubes. The common thread is the intersection of theory and creativity: getting the technical details right, then using them as a foundation to make something unique.
